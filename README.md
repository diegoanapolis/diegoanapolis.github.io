# Research page of Diego Mendes de Souza

Source of <https://diegoanapolis.github.io>, the personal research page of Diego Mendes de Souza, Forensic Expert (Perito Criminal) at the Criminalistics Institute of the Civil Police of the Federal District (IC-PCDF), Brazil.

- `pt.html`: Portuguese version
